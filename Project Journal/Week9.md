## Week 9

We divided up the tasks that we need to get done, and I ended up taking responsibility for the landing page. 
I was honestly pretty excited about this because I've been wanting to get more involved in the actual coding side of the project. Yay!

I started working on the landing page by looking at the existing website and trying to figure out how to make my page fit into the visual direction 
we had already established. 
I wanted it to feel consistent with the rest of the website without just copying the existing layout. 
I started with the basic structure, figuring out where the introduction, credits, and season navigation would go,
and then moved on to styling things like the background texture, vignette, image placement, and overall layout. 
I also started experimenting with making the season images clickable so they can eventually serve as navigation to the different pages.

The whole process has definitely involved a bit of a learning curve. 
I had never used Next.js before, and while I have experience with coding, 
getting familiar with a new framework and understanding how everything in the project fits together took some time. 
I also wanted to make sure I wasn't messing with anything my teammates had already built, 
so there was a bit of figuring things out as I went. 
I am trying to approach it one step at a time rather than getting ahead of myself and attempting to build everything at once.

The landing page is absolutely not finished or final yet, and there are still plenty of things I want to experiment with and improve, 
but I think I have a pretty solid start considering this is my first time working with Next.js. 
I'm happy with the progress I've made so far, especially because 
I'm finally getting to do some actual coding on the project rather than just planning and discussing what we want to make. 
I'm also really looking forward to seeing what everyone else has been working on and how all our individual pieces will eventually come together. 
It's nice to feel like we're moving into the building stage of the project!
