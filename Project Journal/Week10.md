## Week 10 

This week was mostly about continuing to refine the landing page and adding a little more life to it. 
I added the animation for the four season images, which now slowly orbit around a shared central point while staying upright. 
I think it adds some movement and makes the landing page feel a bit less static without being too distracting. 
I also fixed the season names so they appear on top of their respective images rather than awkwardly sitting underneath them. Small changes, 
but they make a difference!

I also experimented with adding a faded effect around the edges of the images to make them blend more into the background. 
Unfortunately, after trying it out, I decided to scrap that idea because it didn't really match the rest of the website's aesthetic,
which VK wants to give more of a sterile clean feel.

For next week, I want to start replacing the placeholder images with the actual seasonal images and finally get rid of all that beautiful, extremely informative lorem ipsum. 
Hopefully VK will have given us the information we need by then so we can start putting in the real content. We also want to work on making the landing page editable through an editor, 
similar to the one Michael built for the season pages. 
That way, the landing page won't be something we have to manually dig into the code to update every time, 
like if information changes, new team members join, or if they want to announce events.

Another idea we've been discussing is adding a transition from the landing page into the current season. 
The four season images will act as buttons for navigating to each individual season, 
but we'd also like to experiment with a smoother transition that takes visitors directly into whichever season matches the actual time of year. 
I think that could be a really nice touch and make the whole experience feel more connected to the changing seasons.

Overall, I'm happy with how the landing page is coming along. 
It's nice to be able to experiment with little details, see them actually working, and occasionally realize that something 
I thought would look great absolutely does not. Progress! Progress! Progress!!!
